FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions.

Find answers to common questions about our internship programs, application process, and selection criteria.

Who can apply?+
Anyone passionate about building real-world projects can apply. We welcome students from all years and disciplines.
Can college students apply?+
Yes, our internships are designed to be manageable alongside college coursework.
Are beginners allowed?+
Yes! We have beginner-friendly programs where mentors will guide you.
Is the internship remote?+
Most of our internships are fully remote. Check the specific internship details for mode of work.
How long is the internship?+
Durations vary, but typically range from 1 to 3 months.
Is there a certificate?+
Yes, successful interns receive a verified completion certificate and a letter of recommendation.
Will I work on real projects?+
Absolutely. You will be assigned to live startup projects to gain real industry exposure.
How does the selection process work?+
After you apply, we review your application. If shortlisted, you will be invited for a quick interview.
How will I know my application status?+
You can track your application status anytime using your Application ID on the Track Application page.
