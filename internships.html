FAQ

Common questions, answered.

Do I need prior coding experience?+
No. Hyna Internship Portal tracks start from fundamentals and gradually move into projects, hackathons, and placement readiness.
Are courses project based?+
Yes. Every track includes mini projects, weekly challenges, reviews, and portfolio outcomes.
Will mentors guide me?+
Mentors help with direction, review, debugging mindset, and interview preparation.
Can I join multiple tracks?+
Yes, but we recommend one primary track and one supporting track for consistent progress.
