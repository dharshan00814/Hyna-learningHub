FAQ

Events questions, answered.

Are HYGEN events beginner friendly?+
Yes. Events are designed with guided participation, mentors, and clear instructions for students at different levels.
Do I receive certificates?+
Eligible participants receive certificates after attendance and completion of the event activity or submission.
Can I attend online?+
Many HYGEN talks and workshops are online or hybrid. Event cards show the exact location format.
Do hackathons require a team?+
Teams are encouraged, but solo participants can join and find team members during community matching.
